This site keeps analytics deliberately simple.

## What is collected

I use [GoatCounter](https://www.goatcounter.com/), a privacy-friendly analytics tool. It records aggregate page views, referrers, and clicks on the links on this page, so I can see which products and links are useful.

GoatCounter does not use cookies, does not store IP addresses, does not build profiles, and does not track visitors across other websites.

## What is not collected

- No cookies or advertising identifiers
- No personal information such as names, emails, or accounts
- No cross-site tracking or data sold to third parties

## Links to other sites

Links to SplitX, Bookmark of the Day, Env Guard, cctrack, and my writing are external. Once you follow them, the privacy policies of those sites apply.
